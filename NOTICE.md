### Ontologies and Reference Data

The Core ontologies reference established external vocabularies including
ADMS, DCAT, Dublin Core, FOAF, OPM, QUDT, Schema.org, SKOS, vCard RDF and W3C
ORG. These external ontologies are referenced by IRI and are not redistributed
as part of this repository. The supporting VSO/ISOProps profile also references ISOProps by IRI.

The ISO 3166 reference datasets use data extracted from EU Vocabularies. Their
source, extraction version and license are documented in the corresponding
`dataset.yaml` and `README.md` files. Every included Construct-X module and
reference dataset records its source and license in its local metadata.

### NUTS 2 regions

The [NUTS 2 vocabulary](datasets/reference/administrative-territories/nuts-2/data/nuts-2-regions.ttl)
is distributed under EPL-2.0. Its region codes and names come from the Gaia-X
Service Characteristics `NUTS2Region` enumeration, release `v2.3.0`.
Construct-X represents them as RDF resources with typed identifiers and country links.
The [dataset documentation](datasets/reference/administrative-territories/nuts-2/README.md#license-and-attribution)
identifies the exact source and accompanies the data in the release alongside the
[full EPL-2.0 license text](datasets/reference/administrative-territories/nuts-2/LICENSE-EPL-2.0.txt).
